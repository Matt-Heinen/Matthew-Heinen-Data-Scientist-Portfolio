# Advertising Cost of Living Improvements Through Childcare Costs 

This project displays visualization skills and the ability to tell a story through data. In this project we evaluate the cost of living across the country and use visualizations to both identify and advertise to family’s why they should move to a different market for a better quality of life. 

## Description

This project is in exercise in data wrangling and visualization pulling data from childcare cost reports as well as income from the National Database for Childcare Prices. Additionally it is an exercise in feature engineering as many features were built in order to help tell the story of some places in the country may be better for quality of life and cost of living then others. Through this analysis visualizations, advertisements and short form videos were created using visualizations to tell the story of convincing families to move to different markets for a better quality of life. Additionally in the paper there is an analysis of the purpose of each visualization or medium as well as a discussion on the effectiveness of each on the reader/viewer.
